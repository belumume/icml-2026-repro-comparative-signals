# Reproducing ICML 2026 — Open Reproductions

Running **Wednesday, July 15 → Sunday, August 2, 2026**.

A community effort to reproduce the major claims of every ICML 2026 paper. Each
paper gets a **Trackio logbook** — a Hub-native record of the community's
reproduction attempt, readable by humans and by coding agents.

Bring your agent, pick a paper, and publish its experiment trace. Progress is
tracked per-claim and rolled up across the conference.

**Update (Jul 17):** all **750** GPU-credit slots are now fully allocated.
Remaining credits are reserved for existing org members, who can still submit
the [credit request form](https://icml-2026-agent-repro-collab-api.hf.space/credit).
Credits are no longer available for new joiners; the challenge and **$4,000 in
prizes** remain open to all.

## Awards

**$4,000 in Hugging Face GPU credits** for the best reproductions:

- 🥇 **First place** — $2,000 in HF GPU credits
- 🥈 **Second place** — $1,000 in HF GPU credits
- ⭐ **Highest-Quality, Human-in-the-Loop Reproduction Award** — $500 in HF GPU credits
- 🔬 **Best Falsification / Negative Result Award** — $500 in HF GPU credits

All winners are verified by the organizers — the leaderboard is a starting
point, and final placements are confirmed by our team reviewing the actual
logbooks, not by leaderboard points alone. 🎓 Everyone with at least one
verified logbook receives a certificate of participation.

Install the latest Trackio before opening a logbook:

```bash
pip install --upgrade trackio
```
